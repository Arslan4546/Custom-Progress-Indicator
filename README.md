# Custom Animated Progress Indicator for Flutter

A beautifully designed, customizable, and easy-to-integrate animated progress indicator for Flutter applications. This package allows you to enhance your Flutter apps with a smooth, visually appealing progress indicator, giving your users a better loading experience. Fully customizable in terms of color, size, speed, and animation style, this progress indicator can be tailored to suit any app’s design aesthetics.

## Features

- **Customizable Animation**: Control the animation's speed, size, and style to align with your app's theme.
- **Multiple Animation Styles**: Choose from several predefined animation styles including rotate, scale, bounce, and more.
- **Smooth & Efficient**: The animations are fluid and optimized for performance on both iOS and Android devices.
- **Lightweight**: The package is small and doesn’t add unnecessary bloat to your app.
- **Easily Integrate**: Plug-and-play integration, with minimal setup required to get started.
